# Product Overview

## Purpose

OpsCore is a retail operations and compliance management platform for small and medium-sized retail businesses.

The goal is to help retail teams manage products, vendors, inventory, purchase orders, inventory movements, and compliance records in one system.

## Target Users

- Business owners
- Store managers
- Inventory staff
- Compliance or administrative staff

## MVP Scope

The first MVP should support:

- Product management
- Vendor management
- Inventory tracking
- Purchase order workflow
- Inventory movement history
- License and compliance record tracking

## Out of Scope for MVP

The first MVP will not include:

- Mobile applications
- Offline mode
- Multi-store support
- Accounting integrations
- Payment processing
- Advanced analytics
- Automatic desktop updates

## Core Workflows

### Purchasing

Vendor -> Purchase Order -> Receive Goods -> Inventory Update -> Inventory Movement Record

### Inventory

Product -> Current Stock -> Stock Adjustment -> Movement History

### Compliance

License or Permit -> Issue Date -> Expiration Date -> Status Tracking
